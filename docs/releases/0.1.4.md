- **应用内更新**：有新版本时 ChillTalk 会提醒你，一键下载、安装并重启，不用再去 GitHub 下载、拖拽替换。菜单栏「检查更新...」可以手动检查，设置 → 概览里可以关掉每天的自动检查。**这一次仍需手动下载安装**，之后的版本都能在应用内更新
- **录音自动结束，不再白白计费**：开始录音后 30 秒没说话会自动取消；说过话后静音 1 分钟会自动结束并照常识别；单次录音最长 10 分钟（千问「说完再识别」为 4 分 50 秒，不会再录完才报错），最后 30 秒在提示框里倒计时

- **In-app updates**: ChillTalk tells you when a new version is out and downloads, installs and relaunches it in one click, so there's no more downloading and dragging from GitHub. Check by hand from the menu bar (Check for Updates…), or turn off the daily check under Settings → Overview. **This update still has to be installed by hand**; later ones install from inside the app
- **Recordings end on their own, so they don't run up a bill**: nothing said in the first 30 seconds cancels the recording; a minute of silence after speaking stops and transcribes it; a recording lasts at most 10 minutes (4:50 for Qwen after-you-finish, which no longer fails once you've finished), with the last 30 seconds counted down in the HUD
